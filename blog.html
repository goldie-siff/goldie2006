my essay

As a young adult in this day and age, technology has shaped my life. Although it hasn't directly affected the kind of person I have grown up to become, it has grown up alongside me and allowed me to make important choices in my life, which has shaped me into who I am today. Like, for example, right now I am writing this essay, and instead of having my buddy ChatGPT write it for me, I have chosen to write it all on my own, which will allow me to grow as a writer and ultimately as a person. In Big data: shaping knowledge, shaping everyday life, by Ralph Schroeder, he argues that big data does not sit in the background, and does in fact shape how we learn, make decisions, and understand ourselves. So, while the growth of technology isn’t the reason why I am who I am today, it has affected aspects of my life, which has shaped me into the person writing this right now.

Obviously writing this essay on my own wasn’t a hard choice for me to make, as over the past few years it has been drilled into my head that using AI is ‘bad’ and I should learn how to succeed on my own. While I do agree that as a college student I should be able to write an essay without the usage of AI, I tend to disagree with the common notion that AI is ‘bad’. Schroeder also argues that the growth of big data and digital systems and cultivates an environment for it to grow. And, as time goes on iit has become crystal clear that technology is only going to continue to grow and prosper, so instead of growing and prospering alongside it, why can’t we grow with it? In my opinion, remixing, reweirding, and reinventing the internet is a great usage of AI and digital technologies. I am able to take inspiration from digital resources and use things like AI and coding to make a message that resonates with me stand out more. While for this project I didn’t use AI (I am unfortunately not that technologically advanced), I did use other technology such as coding to ‘reweird’ the internet and create a website that reveals who I am and what is important to me, which ultimately allowed my creativity to prosper.

As someone who struggles with creativity, I do think that the growth of technology has allowed me to become more creative. I have maybe made like 7 pieces of art in my whole life, but, because of technology I was able to create my first ever piece of ASCII art. I created it using Google Docs, and followed instructions from the web, so thanks technology! In all seriousness, by letting my creativity prosper, I created a piece of art that revolves around one of my biggest passions, soccer. Like technology, soccer has shaped my whole life. I’d say my life revolves around soccer 10x more than it revolves around technology. Nonetheless, my piece of ASCII art is very important to me because it shows two things that have shaped me into who I am today (soccer and technology), while reflecting the modern world I live in.
